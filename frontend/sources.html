Sources

Where the news comes from.

How We Use Sources

Dengbêj AI discovers news through the public RSS feeds of established international broadcasters. We use those feeds to find stories, then summarize and translate them. We do not copy or republish the full articles, and every story keeps a link back to the publisher that reported it.

Current Sources

  • BBC News International news from the British Broadcasting Corporation. Used for story discovery via its public world-news feed, with links to the original reporting.
  • Deutsche Welle (DW) Germany's international broadcaster. Its English feed is used for discovery, always attributed and linked to the original article.
  • Al Jazeera International news network. We use its feed for discovery only, never displaying its article text, and always linking to the source.

Attribution & Fair Use

  • The source is always named and linked.
  • Full source articles are never republished.
  • AI-generated summaries are never presented as original journalism.
  • Summaries are intended to stay grounded in the source, but AI can make mistakes. Check the linked article for important details.

Adding Sources

More sources may be added over time. We aim to check each source's feed and reuse terms before adding it. If you spot a problem with a source, please use the Contact page.
