Privacy

What we collect, and what we deliberately do not.

In Short

Dengbêj AI is a no-login radio. You do not create an account, and we do not ask for your name, email, or any personal detail to listen. We do not set advertising cookies, run analytics trackers, or embed third-party ad scripts.

What We Collect

The site itself asks for no personal information. Your language choice (English or Kurmanji) is stored only in your browser's session storage so the page remembers it while you visit; it never leaves your device and is cleared when you close the tab.

No Tracking or Advertising

  • No analytics or measurement scripts.
  • No advertising or marketing cookies.
  • No third-party trackers, pixels, or embedded ad networks.
  • No forms that transmit your data anywhere.

Third-Party Content

Audio clips are served from AWS storage, and the page loads news data from the project's own API. When you follow a link to an original news source (for example BBC, DW, or Al Jazeera), that publisher's own privacy practices apply once you leave this site.

Hosting

The site is hosted on AWS Amplify. Like any web host, AWS may process standard technical request information (such as IP address) to deliver the page. Dengbêj AI does not use that information to profile or identify you.

Changes & Contact

If this notice changes, the updated version will appear on this page. If advertising or sponsorship is ever added, it will be clearly labelled and this notice will be updated first. Questions about privacy can be raised through the Contact page.
