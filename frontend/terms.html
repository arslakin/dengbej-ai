Terms of Use

Plain terms for an experimental project.

What Dengbêj AI Is

Dengbêj AI is an experimental, non-commercial Kurdish news radio. It automatically curates world news and retells it as short Kurmanji Kurdish broadcasts with English narration as a fallback. It is offered freely, with no account, subscription, or payment.

AI-Assisted Content

Summaries and broadcast scripts are generated with AI assistance. They are not original journalism and can contain errors or omissions. Always treat the original source, which is linked for every story, as the authoritative version. See Transparency for how content is produced and corrected.

Provided As-Is

The service is provided on an "as-is" and "as-available" basis, without warranties of any kind. Availability, freshness, and audio coverage may change as the project evolves. Nothing here is professional, legal, financial, or medical advice.

Acceptable Use

  • Listen, share links, and learn about the dengbêj tradition.
  • Do not attempt to disrupt, overload, or misuse the service.
  • Respect the copyright of the original news publishers when you follow their links.

Sources & Attribution

News is discovered through publicly available RSS feeds and always attributed to its source with a link to the original article. Full source articles are never republished. Details are on the Sources page.

Advertising

There is currently no advertising or sponsorship. If either is added in future, it will be clearly labelled as such and will never influence which stories are selected or how they are summarized.

Changes & Contact

These terms may be updated as the project develops; the current version always lives on this page. Questions can be raised through the Contact page.
