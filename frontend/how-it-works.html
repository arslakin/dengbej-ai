Pipeline Timing

Every 6 hours, fresh articles are ingested from international RSS feeds. Twice daily at 06:00 and 18:00 UTC, the AI curator selects the five most important stories for the day's briefing.

Fifteen minutes after curation, the story processor generates summaries and Kurdish translations. The program generator then classifies stories into regional programs and creates broadcast scripts. Finally, the audio pipeline synthesizes English narration and stores it for streaming.

The entire pipeline is orchestrated by Amazon EventBridge schedules, with each stage running as an independent Lambda function. No servers to manage, no infrastructure to maintain.
