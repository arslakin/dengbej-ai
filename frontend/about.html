About Dengbêj

The story behind the storyteller.

What is a Dengbêj?

A dengbêj is a traditional Kurdish oral storyteller. For generations, dengbêjs have been the voice of their communities — recounting histories, delivering news from distant villages, and preserving collective memory through song and spoken word.

The tradition is especially strong in the Kurmanji-speaking regions. Without written archives, these storytellers carried entire histories in their voices, performing in gathering houses called "dîwanxane" where communities came together to listen.

Today, as the Kurdish language faces challenges in many regions, the dengbêj tradition stands for both cultural resistance and continuity — a reminder that stories survive through telling.

What Dengbêj AI Is

Dengbêj AI is a Kurdish news radio. Each day it gathers world news and retells it as short broadcasts in Kurmanji Kurdish, so you can simply press play and listen — no account, no sign-up.

The retellings are AI-assisted summaries, not original reporting, and every story links back to the publisher that reported it. You can read more about how this works, and how mistakes are corrected, on the Transparency page.

Listening in Kurmanji and English

Selected programs are narrated in Kurmanji. When a Kurmanji recording is unavailable, the player offers English narration if one exists. Some programs may have no audio yet.
