What is a Dengbêj?

For centuries, dengbêjs have preserved Kurdish history through oral storytelling — sharing news, memories, and culture through spoken narratives that connect communities. Dengbej AI transforms this tradition using generative AI, creating Kurdish radio-style programs from current events.
