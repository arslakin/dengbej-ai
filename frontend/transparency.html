Transparency

How Dengbêj AI works, in plain language.

AI-Assisted Summaries

Dengbêj AI listens to the day's news and retells it. The summaries and spoken scripts you hear are written with the help of AI, not by a newsroom of journalists. This lets a small project cover a lot of ground, but it also means the retelling is a summary — not original reporting — and it can get things wrong.

Always Linked to the Source

Every story points back to the original article from the publisher that reported it. If something matters to you, follow the link and read the source. We never republish a publisher's full article, and we never present a summary as if it were original journalism. The Sources page lists where the news comes from.

Mistakes Happen

Because the summaries and translations are AI-generated, they can contain errors, miss context, or phrase something awkwardly in Kurmanji. We would rather be honest about that than pretend otherwise. When you see a mistake, the original source is the version to trust.

Corrections

If you spot an error, please tell us through the Contact page. Clear, specific reports help most — the story, what was wrong, and what it should say. We review reports and fix or remove content that is inaccurate.

Editorial Independence

Which stories appear is decided by the curation process on the day's news, guided by significance and variety. There is currently no advertising or sponsorship. If either is ever added, it will be clearly labelled and will never decide which stories are chosen or how they are told.

Kurdish and English Audio

Selected programs are narrated in Kurmanji Kurdish. When a Kurmanji recording is unavailable, the player offers English narration if one exists. Some programs may have no audio yet.
